## Legenda do post
🧊 A glacier melted... and revealed a murder scene 5,300 years old 😳

📼 This retro-style motion piece tells the story of Ötzi. In September 1991, two hikers found a body emerging from melting ice in the Ötztal Alps, near the Austria–Italy border. At first they thought it was a recently deceased mountaineer. The truth was far more extraordinary: he had lived around 3300 BCE 🏔️

🔬 The glacier had preserved his clothing, leather footwear, tools, weapons, and even traces of his last meals, giving scientists an unmatched look at life in the Copper Age 🪓

🏹 But then came the disturbing part. An arrowhead embedded in his left shoulder showed he had died violently. Who attacked him? Why? And what happened in his final hours? 🔍

🎞️ Built with a vintage documentary look: archival-inspired imagery, frozen landscapes, vintage textures, animated typography, and atmospheric transitions that build toward a mystery preserved in ice
