## Legenda do post
They’re boring 🫠 because they lack contrast 🎯🔥

🎬 This motion piece teaches contrast through the edit itself. Instead of a narrator explaining design principles over unrelated visuals, every concept plays out on screen. Say size, and you see small versus large. Say speed, and you see slow versus fast. Say density, and you see busy versus empty 👀

⚡ The animation becomes the lesson. It covers size, color, speed, shape, and density with clean, intentional visuals, and no unnecessary effects or transitions just to keep the screen busy

🎨 One saturated element becomes the focal point in an otherwise muted frame, and one dramatic size change instantly sets the hierarchy

💡 The core principle is simple: contrast creates attention
