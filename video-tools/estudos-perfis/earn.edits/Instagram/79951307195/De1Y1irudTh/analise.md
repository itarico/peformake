## Legenda do post
🫠 He cut off part of his own ear 👂 ... and then painted The Starry Night 🌌

📼 This retro-style motion piece tells the story of Vincent van Gogh, a man drowning in pain who somehow turned it into some of the most beautiful art ever created. In 1888, after a violent argument with Paul Gauguin, he mutilated part of his left ear. Later he voluntarily entered the Saint-Rémy asylum, where he kept creating some of his most celebrated works 🖼️

🎞️ Built with that vintage throwback look: archival-inspired imagery, expressive typography, animated brushstrokes, and atmospheric transitions. Fragmented compositions reflect his emotional turmoil, while flowing movements echo his distinctive brushwork 🌀

💭 Great storytelling isn’t simply about showing what happened. It’s about making people feel the story
