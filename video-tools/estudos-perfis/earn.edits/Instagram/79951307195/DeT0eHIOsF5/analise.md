## Legenda do post
💎In this tutorial, we turn an ordinary 2D image into a dynamic 2.5D scene using the Parallax Effect in After Effects. Separate the foreground, subject, background, and environment into layers, then push them apart on the Z-axis to build real depth ✨

🎥 Then comes the magic: a 3D camera and a Null Object controller for smooth moves and natural perspective shifts. Objects closer to the camera move differently from those farther away, so the image feels like a living 3D world 🔥

⚙️ You’ll also cover composition views, camera positioning, smooth keyframes, easing, subtle rotations, and scale adjustments to polish the final animation

💡 The goal isn’t just moving a camera through layers. It’s a scene that feels dimensional, cinematic, and alive
