## Legenda do post
🧼 Doctors were unknowingly killing their own patients. One man found the habit that could stop it 😱

📼 This retro-style motion piece tells the story of Ignaz Semmelweis. In the 1840s, mothers were dying from childbirth fever at alarming rates in hospitals meant to save them. He noticed doctors moving straight from autopsies to maternity wards without disinfecting their hands, and he suspected something was being carried between them 🏥

🧪 His fix was remarkably simple: handwashing with a chlorinated solution at Vienna General Hospital. Maternal deaths dropped dramatically. But instead of being celebrated, he faced resistance from a medical establishment that struggled to accept its own hands could be to blame 😔

🎞️ Built with that vintage throwback look: archival imagery, medical illustrations, bold typography, and dramatic transitions carrying the story from discovery to rejection

⚡ Years later, germ theory explained why his approach worked. Sometimes the most important breakthroughs are as simple as washing your hands
