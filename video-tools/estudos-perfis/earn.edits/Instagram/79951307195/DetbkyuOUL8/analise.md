## Legenda do post
🪥 The toothbrush was invented in a JAIL CELL 😳 

📼 This retro-style motion piece tells the story of William Addis. In 1780, while imprisoned in England, he noticed that the way people cleaned their teeth was inefficient and inconvenient. So he drilled holes into a small piece of bone, inserted animal bristles, and made a simple cleaning tool that later became a commercially produced toothbrush 🦷

🎞️ Built with that vintage throwback look: archival-inspired imagery, animated illustrations, bold typography, and detailed object visualizations carrying the journey from a prison cell to an everyday essential

💭 A tool used by billions of people began with one man trying to solve a simple problem in an unlikely place. Sometimes innovation starts with one simple question: “There has to be a better way”
