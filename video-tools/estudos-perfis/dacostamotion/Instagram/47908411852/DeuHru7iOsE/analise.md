## Legenda do post
Motion Design for Idea.ai 🔥

Once again, the legend @socoloff_alex invited me to help bring Idea.ai’s new brand identity to life through motion, and I couldn’t be more stoked with how it turned out.
This project pushed me out of my comfort zone. 

I explored a bunch of new techniques, blending a sleek, tech-driven look with a more raw, handcrafted texture. 

I also took a shot at storyboarding parts of the piece and creating the sound design, which made the whole process even more fun.

Swipe through for a peek at the process and some details behind the animations 👇
